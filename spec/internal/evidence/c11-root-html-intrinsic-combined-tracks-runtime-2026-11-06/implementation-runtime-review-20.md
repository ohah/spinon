During the review, two issues were corrected: the new named-area negative initially expected a `grid-auto-rows` diagnostic, while runtime returned the more specific `grid-template-areas` diagnostic; and layout preflight omitted `max-content` in the max arm of `minmax()`. The diagnostic expectation was corrected, and the intrinsic predicate was brought into agreement with projection and covered by the text matrix. The four composition negatives and the expanded text matrix pass. No unresolved candidate-specific issue was found in this 20-point pass.

Focused layout/style/runtime validation, `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, and scoped all-target Clippy passed. Broad crate runs still have failures; this review does not label them as baseline without a separate base-commit comparison. This bounded candidate remains pending the actual PR-diff review and merge; it does not complete C11.4/C11.
